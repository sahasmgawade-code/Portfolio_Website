Census 2027 — India's First Fully Digital Census

September 2026 · PromptWars × ADYPU Hackathon — 3rd of 200+ builders

An AI-driven digital census portal, designed and shipped end-to-end within a 4-hour hackathon build window using Google Antigravity.

  • Built "Saamvad AI," a conversational assistant supporting up to 10 major Indian languages, powered by Google Gemini.
  • Shipped a self-enumeration and rumor-buster portal with live demographic data visualizations.
  • Included an admin dashboard, phase explorer, and state-wise schedule map for census officials.
  • Live-demoed to a judging panel and secured 3rd rank out of 200+ builders.
