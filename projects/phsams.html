Project 02

Present Hoon Sir — Attendance Management System

Jun – Jul 2026 · In production at ADYPU

A full-stack, multi-batch attendance system with role-based access for Super Admin, Admin, Faculty and Student.

  • QR-based attendance with a variable validity window, one-response-per-device enforcement, and Wi-Fi authentication to prevent proxy marking.
  • Automated email alerts to parents on absences, with exportable PDF/Excel reports.
  • JWT-based auth with CSRF protection and rate limiting across all portals.
